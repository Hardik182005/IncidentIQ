How it works

Alert to resolution.
Under 5 minutes.

IncidentIQ automates the slowest, most stressful parts of incident response so your engineers fix problems, not spreadsheets.

01 — DETECT
Anomaly detected in <1 second
Continuous monitoring across error rates, latency, and service topology. The moment anything diverges from baseline, an incident is opened and your team is alerted. No manual threshold tuning needed.
02 — DIAGNOSE
Root cause in 5.1 seconds
Three AI models run in parallel over your logs, metrics, and dependency graph. Groq identifies the anomaly type, Gemini correlates with recent deploys, OpenAI synthesizes a confidence-scored root cause.
03 — RESOLVE
Risk-rated fix, ready to run
Prioritized remediation commands — labeled LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH risk — generated from your specific incident context. Copy to clipboard, open a PR, or fire a Slack alert. Average MTTR: 4 minutes 32 seconds.
